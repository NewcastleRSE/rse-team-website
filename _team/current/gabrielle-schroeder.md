Gabrielle joined the team as a data scientist in September 2023. After undergraduate training in molecular biology and genetics, she transitioned to research in computational neuroscience as a PhD student and postdoctoral researcher at Newcastle University. Her work focused on developing pipelines and applying a variety of techniques, such as time series analysis and unsupervised learning, to analyse brain activity and clinical data.

Since joining the team, Gabrielle has gained skills in front-end development and user interface/user experience design. Her projects have included designing data analysis workflows on AWS and developing software for biomedical data analysis. She particularly enjoys combining her skills across data science, engineering, and design to create intuitive visualisations and applications that help people understand complex data.
