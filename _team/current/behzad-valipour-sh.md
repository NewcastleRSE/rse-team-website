Behzad is a senior software engineer and data and machine learning specialist with a background in geospatial data science and environmental research. He completed a PhD in Environmental Epidemiology at the Swiss Tropical and Public Health Institute and the University of Basel, where he developed spatio-temporal machine learning models to predict airborne pollen concentrations across Switzerland. He has since worked across academia and industry, applying machine learning and remote sensing to projects including heat-exposure modelling, forest-damage detection, satellite image processing, and environmental monitoring.

At Newcastle University, Behzad leads a Research Software Engineering team developing scalable geospatial data pipelines and research tools. His work spans data engineering, machine learning, cloud and high-performance computing, using technologies including Python, Azure, Docker, and geospatial software. He specialises in building end-to-end systems that turn large and complex datasets into reproducible pipelines, models, APIs, visualisations, and user-facing research tools.
