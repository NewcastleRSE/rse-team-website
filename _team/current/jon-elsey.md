Jon joined the RSE team in November 2025, working as part of the Imago team; developing the UK's Data Service for Imagery. He holds a Masters degree in Physics and a PhD in atmospheric science, and worked as a postdoctoral researcher before shifting to working as an RSE. He joined Newcastle from the University of Leeds, where he worked on optimisation of the next-generation UK Met Office Momentum weather and climate modelling framework. Jon's current work focuses on development and optimisation of data processing pipelines for satellite data, converting huge numbers of pixels into data products that are useful, usable and used by the wider community, with a focus on lowering barriers to entry for social scientists to use imagery.

Jon's experience is primarily in Python and Fortran - leveraging various frameworks such as MPI, OpenMP, Dask, Numba, f2py and CuPy to make code parallel and performant. In addition to work on Imago, Jon is a qualified Carpentries instructor and has been involved in supervision of undergraduate and postgraduate students.
